# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

This policy covers the "Capitol Capital" research tool and its Meta app. The tool is a private, personal project run by one person. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## What the app does

The app reads **public** posts made by the official social media accounts of members of the U.S. Congress. On Instagram it uses Meta's Business Discovery feature, which only returns public Business and Creator accounts. These posts are used for personal research on public policy and markets.

## What information it collects

- Only content that the account owner has already made public: captions, post dates and post links from public Instagram Business or Creator accounts of members of Congress.
- It does **not** collect information from anyone who uses or logs into the app, because the app has no users other than its owner.
- It does **not** read private accounts, direct messages, followers or follower lists, or any personal account.
- It does **not** collect contact details, location, device data or payment data.

## How the information is used

Public posts are saved so that they can be searched and compared with other public information, such as congressional financial disclosures. The data is used only by the owner for personal research.

## Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is not used for advertising.

## Storage and security

Saved posts are kept in private storage that only the owner can open. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published.

## Retention and deletion

Public posts are kept for as long as the research project runs. They are deleted when the project ends, or right away if the account owner asks.

To ask for your posts to be removed, or to ask any other question about this policy, open an issue on this repository: https://github.com/e36meister/congress-signals/issues. Data for an account is deleted within 30 days of the request.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
